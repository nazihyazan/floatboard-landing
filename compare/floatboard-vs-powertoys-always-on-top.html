The Core Difference: Window Pinning vs Purpose-Built Cards

PowerToys Always on Top is a window-management utility. When you activate it, it pins whatever application window currently has focus, drawing a thick colored border around it. While functional, it comes with real productivity hurdles:

  • Heavy screen footprint: Pinning Notepad or a Chrome window leaves title bars, menus, toolbars, and scrollbars cluttering your screen space.
  • No clipboard intelligence: PowerToys cannot capture copied text, preview clips, or store frequent snippets.
  • Windows-only restriction: If you work across macOS or Linux alongside Windows, PowerToys is unavailable.
