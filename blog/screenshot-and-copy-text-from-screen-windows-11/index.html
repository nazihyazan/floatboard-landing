TECHNICAL FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions: Windows 11 & macOS

What shortcut triggers instant screenshot capture?
Press Win + Shift + S on Windows 11 or 10. Once you drag your crop area, FloatBoard automatically pulls the clipboard bitmap onto your pinned board.
Can I copy text directly from the screen?
Yes. Select and copy any onscreen text with Ctrl + C, or paste code snippets directly into FloatBoard's integrated scratchpad panel.
Does FloatBoard interfere with the native Windows clipboard history?
No. Win + V history continues to function independently for historical recall, while FloatBoard serves as your focused, pinned foreground working set.
How does FloatBoard handle multi-monitor displays?
FloatBoard remembers its exact monitor position and bounds across reboots. You can keep it floating on your primary display or tuck it into a secondary reference monitor.
