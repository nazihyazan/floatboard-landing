About the FloatBoard blog

This is FloatBoard’s official product blog. We publish practical guides for people working with desktop notes, copied text and image references.

Who publishes these guides

The publisher is FloatBoard, the application featured on this website. These are product guides, not independent reviews. For the app’s source and release history, visit the FloatBoard project and Linux releases.

How the guides are prepared

These guides are drafted with AI assistance using product screenshots and recorded demonstrations supplied by the app owner, FloatBoard product information and linked primary documentation. We keep each guide focused on one task, describe limitations, link to current pricing and downloads, and avoid invented ratings, customer quotations or performance claims.

Corrections and updates

If an instruction does not match your version, contact FloatBoard with the article URL, your operating system and app version.
