The brutal math behind the Alt-Tab tax

Let's do honest math on a routine engineering task: testing an API webhook.

  1. You copy a staging endpoint from your browser docs.
  2. Alt-Tab to your terminal to prepare a curl command.
  3. You need the bearer auth token from Postman. Alt-Tab to Postman, copy the token.
  4. Alt-Tab back to terminal, paste it.
  5. Forgot the exact JSON schema required for the payload? Alt-Tab back to browser.
  6. Alt-Tab back to terminal to run the test.

That was five window switches for a single command. At an average of 3 seconds per transition (including mental re-indexing), that is 15 seconds of pure friction. Multiply that across 150 small debugging cycles a day, and you spend 35 to 50 minutes every day doing nothing except flipping between overlapping windows.

Dual 4K monitors help when you are seated at an office desk, but they introduce neck fatigue, cost hundreds of dollars, and completely disappear when you are working on a 14-inch laptop at a coffee shop, co-working space, or airport.
