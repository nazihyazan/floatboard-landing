Frequently Asked Questions About UX Screenshot Audits

What is the fastest shortcut to capture screen crops into FloatBoard?

Press Win + Shift + S on Windows (or Cmd + Shift + 4 on macOS); FloatBoard automatically detects the new screenshot in your clipboard and docks it into your board.

Can I reorder cards inside the UX moodboard?

Yes. FloatBoard supports intuitive drag-and-drop reordering of both images and text notes.
