Keep Hero Reference Stills Anchored Over DaVinci's Color Page

Matching skin tones, highlight roll-off, and shadow tint across scenes is one of the most demanding tasks in post-production. While DaVinci Resolve provides an internal Gallery Wipe feature, toggling split screens repeatedly interrupts the colorist's visual rhythm and temporarily hides timeline scopes.

FloatBoard allows colorists to float reference stills directly alongside the Vectorscope and Waveform monitor. This persistent reference window enables real-time color balancing on primary color wheels without constantly activating and deactivating wipe overlays.

Compare Moodboard Palettes in Premiere Pro Lumetri Color

In Adobe Premiere Pro, applying creative Look Up Tables (LUTs) through the Lumetri Color panel frequently requires checking client visual guidelines. Toggling back to a PDF deck or browser moodboard introduces cognitive friction and breaks color perception.

By anchoring a curated 4-card reference grid in FloatBoard, video editors can evaluate Lumetri Curves, HSL Secondary selections, and Vignette settings against approved project art directions instantly.

Grade with Reference Grids on a Single Editing Display

Many independent video editors and colorists work on high-color-accuracy laptops (such as Apple MacBook Pro Liquid Retina XDR displays or calibrated OLED workstations) where dual-monitor setups are unavailable during travel or set shoots.

When working on a single display, pairing your stills with an always-on-top floating notes scratchpad keeps client hex codes, revision timestamps, and LUT names right beside your video scopes without blocking essential playback controls.
