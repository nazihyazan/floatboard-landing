Quick Workflow Summary (TL;DR)
  1. Collect your top 4 landscape photo candidates and add them into FloatBoard's image grid.
  2. Position the floating board right over your website header mockup to test text contrast and focal points.
  3. Eliminate weak images one by one until you identify the photo that pairs best with your call-to-action.

Test Headline Readability and CTA Contrast Live

A landscape photo that looks stunning in an image viewer can completely fail as a website hero if high-frequency details (such as trees or snow peaks) clash with your white H1 typography or primary CTA buttons.

By floating candidate hero images directly over your local development server or Figma canvas, FloatBoard lets you test visual contrast and headline legibility in real time before writing a single line of CSS.

Evaluate Focal Points Across Mobile and Desktop Viewports

Modern responsive headers change aspect ratio dramatically from ultra-wide 21:9 monitors to vertical 9:16 mobile screens. FloatBoard allows you to quickly crop and inspect how subject elements behave under different viewport constraints.
