Why Linux Developers & Sysadmins Use FloatBoard

Managing servers, writing shell scripts, and developing software on Linux requires constant copying and cross-referencing. FloatBoard simplifies your workflow:

  • Always-on-Top Command Pad: Keep frequent SSH commands, Docker one-liners, and regex patterns pinned above your terminal.
