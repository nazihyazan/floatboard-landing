Why Upgrade from Traditional Clipboard Managers?

For years, power users on Windows and Linux relied on tools like Ditto and CopyQ. While robust, their interfaces were designed decades ago:

  • Text-only list views: Long lists of text make it difficult to scan copied code snippets, multiline templates, or image screenshots.
  • Transient popups: Traditional clipboards pop up from the system tray, require selection, and immediately close upon losing focus. You cannot keep multiple items in view.
  • No sticky notes or reference board: There is no space to draft notes or arrange visual references alongside your copied history.
