How to keep a note visible while you work

  1. Open FloatBoard. Download the installer for your operating system, launch the app and position the floating board near your current task.
  2. Add your reference. Copy a passage from a document and paste it into the board, or add an image you need to refer to.
  3. Keep the board on top. Use the always-on-top setting and return to your other app. Your reference stays visible without bringing a separate notes window forward each time.
  4. Refresh your workspace. Replace the reference when you change tasks. Keep only the notes you need for the work in front of you.

Example: take research notes beside a draft

You are writing a report and need to refer to a short quotation, its source link and a reference image. Paste the text and link into FloatBoard, then add the image. Move the board to the side of your document so it does not cover the paragraph you are writing.

When you switch between your browser and the draft, the floating board keeps those references in view. This also works for meeting talking points, a translation beside its original text, or instructions you want to follow in another app.

A sticky notes alternative for text and images

If you use desktop sticky notes to keep a reminder nearby, FloatBoard offers a workspace for reference text and images together. Its always-on-top window is useful when your notes would otherwise disappear behind your active application.

For repeated copy and paste tasks, the same app also provides clipboard history and a floating clipboard manager. Use notes for information you want to keep visible and clipboard history to return to recently copied text.

Questions about floating notes

Yes. FloatBoard has downloads for Windows, macOS and Linux. Choose your platform in the download section.

Can a note contain images as well as text?

Yes. The board supports text and images, so a visual reference can sit beside your written notes. Text and image limits depend on your plan.

The free plan includes one window. Premium supports multiple windows when you need separate references around your desktop.
