Frequently Asked Questions

Is FloatBoard really free to download?

Yes. You can download and use FloatBoard Free immediately with no credit card and no expiration. If you ever need unlimited items, multiple boards, and advanced features, you can get the $5.99 one-time lifetime license.

Does FloatBoard require an account or internet connection?

No. FloatBoard is 100% offline. Everything you type, copy, or pin is stored locally on your hard drive. Zero telemetry, zero cloud tracking, zero data leaks.

How does "Always on Top" work?

FloatBoard uses native operating system APIs to stay floating above full-screen editors (VS Code, Cursor), web browsers, and word processors. Read our floating notes guide and clipboard manager guide for more details.
